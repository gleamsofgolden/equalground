Our Story

We believe the personal is still political

Equal Ground was founded in 2026 by writers and editors who were tired of watching intersectional feminist perspectives get sidelined, simplified, or sensationalized. We built a different kind of platform.

Mission

To make equity legible

We publish journalism, essays, and analysis that treats gender not as a theme, but as a lens. A way of seeing power, systems, and possibility that cuts across every issue — from economics to culture, from law to love.

⊜

Core Values

What we stand on

⊜
Equality

Not equality of sameness, but equality of dignity. Every voice in our archive is treated as worthy of the same care, rigor, and platform.

◎
Voice

We amplify perspectives that are structurally underrepresented — not because they're exceptional, but because the systems that silence them are unjust.

⊗
Resistance

Resistance is not opposition for its own sake. It is the refusal to accept that what exists is what must exist. We practice it in every editorial decision.

⊕
Community

This platform belongs to its readers. We build accountability structures into everything — from our editorial process to how we handle comments and contributions.
